📘 CTR Analyzer — Hindi Guide

Yeh tool kya karta hai? Yeh tool aapke ads, YouTube titles, email subject lines ka Click-Through-Rate (CTR) calculate, predict aur improve karne me madad karta hai.

Use kaise karein:

  1. Calculate CTR: Apne campaign ke Impressions aur Clicks daalein, platform select karein, aur "Calculate CTR" dabayein. Aapko turant CTR%, rating (Excellent/Good/Average/Poor) aur industry average se comparison milega.
  2. Predict & Improve: Apna headline ya ad copy paste karein — AI ise analyze karke 0-100 score dega aur improvement ke liye specific suggestions dega (jaise power words, numbers, urgency add karna).
  3. A/B Compare: Do alag headlines daalein, tool dono ko score karke batayega konsa behtar perform karega.
  4. Benchmarks: Har platform (Google, YouTube, Facebook, Email) ka industry-standard average CTR dekh sakte hain.
  5. History: Aapke pichle saare analyses is device par save rehte hain, taaki aap progress track kar sakein.

Free vs Pro: Free plan me aap din me 3 baar "Predict" aur "Compare" use kar sakte hain. Pro unlock karne ke baad unlimited analyses milte hain, lifetime ke liye — ek baar payment karke.
